# Preparation, passage and return

[Project overview](../README.md) · [Selected portfolio](../portfolio/Selected.pdf?raw=true) · [Full case study](../portfolio/Full-case-study.md)

## Preparation belongs somewhere

The repository separates knowledge from commitment. Recovered effects can be read anywhere; changing active powers requires grounded altar proximity outside an active guardian encounter. Returning to a checkpoint therefore supports preparation as well as respawning.

The homepage's Harvest-to-Sun exchange gives up a 20% shop discount for 40% more attack reach. The limit matters: Harvest can be worn for a shop visit and replaced later at an altar. Its cost is an active slot during that part of the route, not permanent loss of the discount or gold. This is not an irreversible class choice or a measured player preference.

## A gate must preserve the way back

[![Unity scene overview with analysis labels for regions and guardian rooms](../media/world-route.jpg)](../media/world-route.jpg?raw=true)

*The room numbers identify guardians; they are not a literal straight-line travel path. This is not the player-facing map screen.*

An early drop can strand a player without the next ability. The revised fractured floor preserves the return route until Shockwave can open it. Approach, landing-triggered encounter, reward and fractured exit form a learning sequence.

Walls and floors share a visible fractured material. A sword is insufficient; the beam breaks successive eligible blocks. Ordinary stone stays solid. Consistency lets the player recognise an earlier barrier after earning the ability instead of relying on a fresh instruction at every gate.

## Growth changes the problem

Abaddon grants Shockwave, Korah Wall Jump, Azazel Double Jump and Bel Crystal Dash. Their uses combine on the return toward Nero rather than remaining isolated demonstrations.

Spirit supports traversal and recovery. The earlier free Dash allowed repeat mobility without sharing that cost; v0.56 adds payment on launch, free cancellation and a release requirement. Unique guardian victories increase capacity by one. Whether the pressure feels fair on a first playthrough remains an observation question; no ideal failure rate or completion time is claimed.

## Show enough to plan

The v0.57 camera keeps a wider view in ordinary exploration and guardian arenas. A dead zone prevents short movement and turns from dragging the scene. Only the authored lower-right moss labyrinth chooses the closer view, with gradual transitions and boundary hysteresis.

Ordinary routes prioritise reading possible paths; narrow maze corridors leave turns uncertain and reward detours. Framing provides that distinction without global black fog. The player-facing map separately reveals explored space.
